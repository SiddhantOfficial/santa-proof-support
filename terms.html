App license

Santa Proof is licensed under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. It is a photo editor for adults creating entertainment images. You are responsible for having permission to use and share photographs you import.

Free and Premium

The free experience includes the starter pose, sample rooms, previews of launch poses, and watermarked exports up to 1,280 pixels. One non-consumable Premium purchase unlocks all launch poses and watermark-free exports up to 4,096 pixels. Output preserves aspect ratio and never upscales a smaller source. Premium is not a subscription and does not expire at Christmas. Future separate content is not promised.

Payments and restoration

Apple displays the price and handles payment. Restore Purchases is available in the app using the purchasing Apple Account. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's support service. Local draft deletion does not cancel or remove purchase ownership.

Your work

Keep important exported photographs in a location you control. Deleting local drafts is irreversible. Photo quality depends on the source, placement, lighting and edits; the app does not promise that every composition will look like an unedited photograph.
