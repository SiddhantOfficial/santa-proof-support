Privacy policy

Your photos.
Your device.

Effective 9 November 2026. This policy describes Santa Proof version 1.0.

Local photo editing

Santa Proof is a parent-operated iPhone photo editor. It does not require an app account and includes no advertising, tracking SDK or third-party analytics SDK. Photo compositing, masks, edit instructions and drafts are processed and stored locally. The app does not upload your photos to a developer-operated server or cloud image service.

Photo access and exports

The system photo picker lets you choose specific images. The app does not request broad read access to your photo library. Saving directly to Photos requests add-only access when required. Sharing is initiated by you through the system share sheet; the destination you select has its own privacy practices. Exported JPEGs omit source GPS and unnecessary source metadata.

Retention and deletion

Local drafts retain a source copy and edit instructions until you delete them. Remove individual projects or all local project data using the app's controls. Deleting the app normally removes its local container. Saved or shared exports are separate copies and must be deleted in their destination. Project storage is excluded from app backup; your photo library and device services remain subject to your Apple settings and policies. The app does not provide developer-operated cloud sync.

Purchases

Apple processes payment and provides verified purchase information through StoreKit to determine Premium ownership. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles account, transaction and diagnostic information under its own policies. Restore Purchases uses Apple's services. Deleting a project does not remove a permanent purchase.

Support and external pages

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide so we can answer. Avoid sending private photographs or sensitive records unless needed for an issue you want us to investigate. The email and website hosting providers may process communications and request logs under their policies. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Families

The app is designed for parents and adults. It does not ask for children's names, birth dates, profiles, voices, contacts or location.

Contact and changes

Publisher and privacy contact: TECHNICAL DOST SOLUTIONS, Raipur, India. Email support@technicaldost.com. If the app's practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.
