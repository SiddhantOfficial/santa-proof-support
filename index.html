Help with Santa Proof, a photo editor for parents. This support page does not announce App Store availability.

Get help

Email support@technicaldost.com with the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, and a description of the problem. You do not need to send a personal photograph. Never send payment card details.

Start with a sample

Choose Try a sample from Home to edit an included room. Or choose a photo from your library using the system picker. Santa Proof edits a local copy and leaves your original untouched.

Make Santa fit

Use Santa to choose a pose and adjust placement. Use Light and Shadow to suit the room. Erase hides parts behind furniture; Restore brings them back. Undo and redo let you revise an edit. Changing to a different pose clears that pose's incompatible mask.

Save or share

Open Preview to compare before and after. The starter pose supports free watermarked exports up to 1,280 pixels on the longest side. Premium supports watermark-free exports up to 4,096 pixels. Smaller originals are never enlarged.

If saving to Photos is denied, allow add-only access in your iPhone's Settings or use Share to choose another destination. An unsuccessful save leaves your draft in the app.

Restore Premium

Choose Restore Purchases in Settings or on the purchase sheet, using the Apple Account that bought Santa Proof Premium. An internet connection may be needed. Premium is one permanent non-consumable unlock, not a subscription. It does not expire at Christmas.

Manage local projects

Reopen a recent project to keep editing. Duplicate it to try another idea. Delete a project, or remove all local project data in Settings, when you no longer need it. Confirmed deletion cannot be undone. Photos you already saved or shared are managed separately in their destination.

About the app

Santa Proof creates edited entertainment photographs. It does not record a real Santa visit, provide live contact with Santa, or generate video. Editing takes place on your device.
